# Positioning

## One line

**Ambient is the CRM that fills itself in** — it turns the customer email and
meetings your account managers already have into an up-to-date account
timeline, a daily to-do list and an assistant that knows every customer.

## Category

Account management CRM for B2B companies with field and inside sales teams
managing an existing customer base (renewals, repeat orders, growth), rather
than top-of-funnel prospecting.

## Ideal customer

| Attribute | Fit |
| --- | --- |
| Business | B2B distributor, manufacturer or wholesaler with repeat-order customers |
| Size | 20–500 account managers |
| Stack | Microsoft 365 (Outlook, Teams); an ERP or data warehouse holding sales history |
| Pain | Existing CRM is empty or out of date; managers chase activity in spreadsheets |
| Buyer | Head of sales or sales operations; IT approves the Microsoft 365 consent |

## Value

| For | Before | With Ambient |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Account manager | Retypes emails into the CRM, or doesn't | Conversations file themselves; follow-ups become tasks with one click |
| Sales manager | Asks "did anyone talk to them?" | Sees real activity and health across the team, without reading private mail |
| Head of sales | Monthly numbers from spreadsheets | One roll-up across teams and divisions, sales against target, risk early |
| IT and security | Worries about mailbox access | Customer-only capture, participant-only content, full audit log, admin-controlled consent |

## Messaging pillars

1. **No more logging.** Customer email and meetings are captured automatically;
   Ambient is up to date because nobody has to update it.
2. **Know what to do next.** A "do first" list, smart tasks from real
   conversations, and a morning brief.
3. **Every customer in one view.** Sales, orders, contracts, contacts and every
   conversation on one timeline.
4. **Private by design.** Only people on a conversation can read it; seniority
   never unlocks someone else's mail.

## How we compare

| | General-purpose CRMs | Conversation-intelligence add-ons | Ambient |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Activity data | Manual entry, optional capture add-ons | Calls and meetings, separate tool | Email and meetings captured into the CRM itself |
| Built for | Pipeline and opportunity tracking | Coaching on calls | Managing and growing an existing book of accounts |
| Customer data | Lives in the CRM | Lives in the add-on | Mirrors the ERP / warehouse; CRM owns activity |
| AI | Bolt-on features | Call analysis | Assistant and tasks grounded in the account's own history |
| Privacy model | Record-level sharing rules | Varies | Account visibility plus participant-only content |

## Voice

Plain, specific and calm. Say what it does ("files the email on the right
account"), not what it is ("AI-powered synergy platform"). No hype words.
